What is Science Olympiad?

Science Olympiad is a competitive team-based program that promotes STEM education through engaging events in biology, earth science, chemistry, physics, and engineering. Students work together to build devices, conduct experiments, and solve problems in tournaments against other schools.

East Lansing High School's Science Olympiad team has a strong history of success at regional and state competitions, with students developing valuable skills while having fun and meeting fellow science enthusiasts.
Why Join Science Olympiad?

Academic Excellence

Enhance your knowledge and skills in science, technology, engineering, and math while building an impressive college application profile.

Teamwork & Friendship

Connect with like-minded peers, develop teamwork skills, and create lasting friendships with students who share your interests.

Hands-On Experience

Design and build devices, conduct experiments, and go beyound the classroom in a fun competition setting.

What Events Can I Participate In?

Science Olympiad offers over 23 diverse events covering every area of science:

  • Biology/Environment: Anatomy and Physiology, Designer Genes, Entomology, Water Quality
  • Earth/Space Science: Astronomy, Dynamic Planet, Remote Sensing, Rocks and Minerals
  • Physics/Chemistry: Circuit Lab, Hovercraft, Machines, Chemistry Lab, Forensics, Materials Science
  • Tech/Engineering: Boomilever, Electric Vehicle, Helicopter, Robot Tour
  • Nature of STEM: Codebusters, Experimental Design, Bungee Drop, Engineering CAD

Most importantly, there's something for everyone regardless of your scientific interests!

Science Olympiad welcomes students of all science backgrounds and experience levels. Many of our most successful members started with little experience but brought enthusiasm and willingness to learn. We strive to keep Science Olympiad manageable and enjoyable for all members. Though there are few official comittments, the time and effort you put in to the team directly influences your success.

That said, the basic commitment during the school year is our weekly Friday meetings from 2:45-3:45 PM. As competitions approach, event teams are expected to arrange additional practice sessions. You can adjust your involvement based on your schedule and interests. Many members balance Science Olympiad with other activities and maintain strong academic performance. Tournaments typically run all day on Saturdays. The day usually begins early, travelling as a team to the hosting institution. After impound of certain constructed devices, event rotations commence where you'll compete in your assigned events with your partners. Events include written tests, lab experiments, or demonstrating devices you've built. The day concludes with an awards ceremony celebrating top performers.
